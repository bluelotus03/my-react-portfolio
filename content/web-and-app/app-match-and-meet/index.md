This was a project I created with a fellow GWC member as part of the Girls Who Code College Loops Challenge. The prompt was "Create a technical solution or product that showcases the GWC value of sisterhood."

Zaina and I decided to make a prototype of an app called Match & Meet, where you can find connections with fellow GWC members at your college loop for various things like events, projects, interests, and courses. We wanted to take those connections a step further to solve some of the issues we faced in chats or other apps. Learn more in our Project Overview below!

🏆 We tied for 3rd place at the national level!
